Surveyor 3

Landed in Oceanus Procellarum on April 20, 1967. Proved lunar soil could bear the weight of Apollo astronauts. In 1969, Apollo 12 landed 180 m away and retrieved its camera for analysis on Earth.

NSSDCA ID: 1967-035A. Mass: 997.9 kg. Landing coords: 3.0° S, 23.41° W. First surface sampler arm on the Moon. Measured regolith bearing strength at ~98 kPa (~10 psi). Returned parts enabled the first material science study of long-term space exposure.

Why it matters

Surveyor 3 paved the way for human boots on the Moon by proving the lunar regolith was firm enough to support an Apollo Lunar Module.
